Strategic Planning - Practice Questions

1. While preparing its mission statement, a company is deciding what information should be included. Which of the following items would be least suitable for inclusion in the mission statement?
Correct Answer: D. Detailed operational or tactical plans do not belong in a high-level mission statement.
2. A business offers a product that serves a broad customer base while being viewed as distinct from competing products. As a result, it consistently earns above-average profits. Which competitive strategy best describes this approach?
Correct Answer: B. Offering a distinct product to a broad market is a Product Differentiation Strategy.
3. According to Porter's Five Forces framework, the combined influence of the competitive forces primarily determines which of the following?
Correct Answer: A. Five Forces analysis evaluates overall industry attractiveness and long-term profit potential.
4. As part of a strategic planning exercise, the management of a food-processing company is reviewing its internal environment. Which of the following is most likely considered an internal strategic factor?
Correct Answer: D. Organizational culture is an internal factor unique to the company.
5. When an organization develops alternative plans to deal with unexpected future events, this process is known as:
Correct Answer: A. Planning for unexpected alternative events is contingency planning.
6. Planning provides several important benefits to an organization. Which of the following is not considered a primary purpose of planning?
Correct Answer: C. Hiring/selection is a specific HR operational function rather than a primary goal of organizational strategic planning.
7. Evaluating a company's technology, workforce capabilities, and effectiveness of its sales force helps identify:
Correct Answer: D. Evaluating internal resources (technology, workforce, sales force) reveals internal strengths and internal weaknesses.
8. A company's unique competencies primarily originate from which of the following?
Correct Answer: B. Distinctive competencies stem directly from internal resources and capabilities.
9. During strategic planning, which of the following should be analyzed as an external environmental factor?
Correct Answer: A. Societal culture and values are macro-environmental (PESTEL) factors external to the firm.
10. A company consistently outperforms its competitors in a highly competitive industry. Which statement best explains its superior performance?
Correct Answer: B. Superior performance is driven by executing strategies that deliver competitive advantages relative to rivals.
11. Strategic analysis generally focuses on long-term objectives and organizational direction. Which of the following would normally be excluded from strategic analysis?
Correct Answer: A. Short-term scheduling for the current year is an operational decision, not a strategic one.
12. Company profitability depends on several fundamental factors. Which of the following is not one of these basic drivers?
Correct Answer: D. Value, Price, and Cost are the core economic drivers of profit margin. R&D is a function/strategy.
13. A company that possesses a sustainable competitive advantage over its rivals is most likely to:
Correct Answer: C. The classic definition of competitive advantage is achieving sustained above-average profitability.
14. Porter's Five Forces Model is designed to evaluate the competitive environment within an industry. Which of the following is not one of the five competitive forces?
Correct Answer: B. The force is "Rivalry among existing competitors," not bargaining power of competitors.
15. To remain competitive and avoid failure, a company should continually focus on all of the following except:
Correct Answer: B. Solely relying on past strategies leads to strategic drift and competitive risk.
16. Which of the following plans outlines an organization's long-term direction, objectives, and position within its business environment?
Correct Answer: B. Strategic plans dictate long-term direction and market positioning.
17. A company has introduced a flashlight with a wireless charging feature that no competitor currently offers. Market research indicates that most customers are willing to pay a premium for this innovation. Which of Porter's competitive strategies would be most appropriate?
Correct Answer: C. Offering unique features that command a premium price across the market is Differentiation.
18. Strategic planning generally involves several key activities. Which of the following is least likely to be considered part of the strategic planning process?
Correct Answer: A. Reviewing departmental operational budgets is part of operational control, not high-level strategy formulation.
19. When beginning the strategic planning process, management should first establish the company's:
Correct Answer: A. Objectives and mission serve as the initial step in strategy development.
20. Which of the following expresses what an organization seeks to achieve and serves as the foundation for its long-term objectives?
Correct Answer: B. The mission statement provides purpose and serves as the benchmark for establishing long-term goals.
21. The overall approach that management adopts to achieve one or more organizational objectives is known as:
Correct Answer: B. Strategy is the overall plan or approach chosen to reach goals.
22. Which of the following is not a characteristic commonly associated with a tactical plan?
Correct Answer: A. Tactical plans are handled by middle managers; strategic plans are driven by top executive management.
23. A company's competitive advantage is built on four key factors that arise from its distinctive competencies. Which of the following correctly identifies these factors?
Correct Answer: A. Efficiency, Quality, Innovation, and Customer Responsiveness are Hill & Jones' 4 building blocks of competitive advantage.
24. Organizations can achieve competitive advantage through several generic competitive strategies. Which of the following is not one of these strategies?
Correct Answer: B. "Innovation" is a driver/building block, not one of Porter's generic competitive business strategies.
25. The primary purpose of a company's mission statement is to define:
Correct Answer: D. A mission statement specifies an organization's core purpose.
26. A premium bottled water company controls 60% of the market and operates in an industry with significant entry barriers. However, supermarkets—the company's largest customers—are becoming larger through consolidation, increasing their bargaining power. Based on Porter's Five Forces, which statement best describes this industry?
Correct Answer: D. High market share and entry barriers support current profitability, but buyer consolidation threatens margins over time.
27. Cerawell Products Company is experiencing several operational challenges. Which of the following situations is least controllable by management during the current fiscal year?
Correct Answer: C. Competitor innovations in the market are external threats that cannot be directly controlled by management.
28. As part of strategic planning, organizations perform an external analysis to identify opportunities and threats. Which of the following is not normally included in an external analysis?
Correct Answer: C. Internal strengths and weaknesses are part of internal analysis, not external analysis.
